# Aurora Labs — Employee Handbook 2026

## About this handbook

This handbook applies to all permanent employees, fixed-term contractors and interns at Aurora Labs (Pty) Ltd, headquartered in Cape Town with a satellite office in Johannesburg. It is reviewed every January by the People team. Where this handbook conflicts with a signed employment contract, the contract takes precedence.

Questions about anything in this document go to people@auroralabs.example.

## Leave and time off

### Annual leave

Permanent employees accrue **25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year**, in addition to public holidays. Leave accrues monthly at 2.08 days per month and is available from the first day of employment. Up to **5 unused days may be carried over** into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March, after which they are forfeited.

Leave requests are made in Aurora Flow under *People → Time off*. Requests longer than five consecutive working days need approval from both the line manager and the People team. During December, the company enforces a **minimum team coverage of 50%** — managers may decline leave that would breach this.

### Sick leave

Employees receive **30 days of paid sick leave per three-year cycle**. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than two consecutive days. Sick leave may not be converted to cash and does not carry over between cycles.

### Parental leave

- **Primary caregiver:** 20 weeks fully paid.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 8 weeks fully paid.
- Adoption and surrogacy follow the same structure as parental leave, effective from the date the child joins the household.
- Employees returning from parental leave may request a phased return (60% hours at full pay) for the first four weeks.

### Study and exam leave

Employees may take **5 days of paid study leave per year** for approved courses related to their role. Approval is at manager discretion. Exam leave is deducted from this allowance.

## Working arrangements

### Remote and hybrid work

Aurora Labs operates a **hybrid-first** model. All employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, with Tuesdays and Thursdays designated as anchor days in the office for teams. Fully remote arrangements require VP approval and a documented home-office assessment.

### Core hours

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–15:00 SAST**, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees may schedule work flexibly, provided they attend meetings and meet deliverables. Employees in other time zones agree core hours with their manager, with at least four overlapping hours with their team.

### Home-office allowance

- **New employees:** R12,000 one-off stipend, payable after probation, for a desk, chair or monitor.
- **Existing employees:** R2,500 annual refresh allowance.
- Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to the employee after 12 months of use.

### Internet and power

The company reimburses **up to R800 per month** for home internet where remote work exceeds 2 days per week. During scheduled load-shedding, employees may claim a **R350 monthly backup-power allowance** without submitting invoices.

## Expenses

All expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of the transaction through Aurora Flow → Finance → Expenses. Receipts are mandatory for any claim above R150.

| Category | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Local accommodation | R2,400 per night |
| International accommodation | R4,500 per night |
| Meals during travel | R650 per day |
| Client entertainment | R1,100 per person, pre-approved by a director |
| Taxi / ride-hailing after 20:00 | Approved for safety, no cap |

Expenses above R10,000 in total per month require an additional finance review. Corporate cards are issued to directors and team leads only.

## Learning and development

Each employee has an annual **L&D budget of R25,000** for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Requests are made in Aurora Flow and auto-approved if the amount is under R5,000 and the course relates to the employee's development plan. Unused L&D budget expires on 31 December and is not paid out.

The company runs **Learning Friday**: the last Friday of each month is reserved for learning, with no internal meetings scheduled.

## Performance and progression

Performance is reviewed twice a year (April and October) using a four-point scale: *Below, Meeting, Exceeding, Outstanding*. Promotion cycles run in **May and November**. Employees must be in their current role for at least 6 months and rated *Exceeding* or above to be eligible for promotion.

Compensation is benchmarked annually against the South African market using a 60th percentile target. Bonuses, where applicable, are paid in December and are discretionary.

## Conduct, security and data

### Device and account security

All laptops must have full-disk encryption and the Aurora endpoint agent installed. Passwords must be at least **14 characters** and managed in the company password manager — reuse is prohibited. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts. Hardware security keys are issued to engineering, finance and executive staff.

### Confidentiality

Customer data may only be processed in systems approved by the Security team. Copying production data to personal devices is prohibited. Any suspected data breach must be reported to security@auroralabs.example **within 1 hour** of discovery; the incident hotline is +27 21 555 0142.

### AI tool usage

Employees may use approved AI assistants (currently Claude via the company workspace) for drafting and analysis. **Customer data must never be pasted into unapproved AI tools.** AI-generated content that is shared externally must be reviewed by a human owner who takes responsibility for accuracy. Use of AI in hiring decisions is not permitted.

## Travel

Domestic travel is booked through the travel desk at least **7 days** in advance; international travel at least **21 days** in advance. Economy class for flights under 6 hours, premium economy for flights over 6 hours with director approval. Travel insurance is arranged automatically.

## Onboarding

New employees receive their laptop by courier **before** their start date. Week 1 includes security training, a product deep-dive and a buddy assignment. Probation is **3 months** for individual contributors and **6 months** for managers. A first performance conversation happens at day 30.

## Leaving Aurora

Notice periods: **30 days** for individual contributors, **60 days** for managers and directors. Exit interviews are conducted by the People team. Leave payouts are limited to the 5 carried-over days described above. Laptops must be returned within 5 working days of the last day.
